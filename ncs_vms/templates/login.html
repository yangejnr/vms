What the system supports

The prototype replaces paper registers with a searchable record of visitors, visits, sign-in status, and visit slips that can be checked at the reception desk.

Visitor Registration

Capture names, contact information, address details, and optional photos for new visitors.

Returning Visitor Lookup

Search by phone or email to reuse existing visitor records and view previous visit history.

Visit Tracking

Create official or personal visit entries with documents carried, group size, and active status.

Daily Movement View

Review today’s visits and sign visitors out when they leave the premises.
